Ask ten Christians to describe the Father, and you’ll hear words like *good*, *loving*, *Creator*. Ask them about Jesus, and you’ll hear about the cross and the resurrection. But ask about the Holy Spirit, and many of us go quiet.

That’s understandable. The Spirit is often called the most mysterious Person of the Trinity. But Scripture tells us a great deal about Him. Here are seven things worth knowing.

## 1. He was there at the very beginning

The second verse of the Bible says, “the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters” (Genesis 1:2). Before light, before land, the Holy Spirit was present, hovering over creation, ready to bring life. The Hebrew word for spirit, *ruach*, also means *breath* and *wind*.

## 2. He is a Person, not a force

The Greek word for spirit, *pneuma*, is grammatically neuter, which is why older translations sometimes say “it” (like “the Spirit itself” in Romans 8:26). But when Jesus describes the Comforter’s work, John deliberately uses the masculine *he*: “he shall teach you all things” (John 14:26), “he will guide you into all truth” (John 16:13). The Spirit teaches, speaks, guides and prays. Paul even writes, “grieve not the holy Spirit of God” (Ephesians 4:30). You can’t grieve electricity. You can only grieve someone who loves you.

## 3. He is “another Comforter,” just like Jesus

On His last night with His disciples, Jesus promised, “I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever” (John 14:16). The Greek word for *another* means another of the same kind. Everything Jesus was to His disciples, the Spirit would continue to be.

## 4. Jesus said it was better that He came

“It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you” (John 16:7). In His earthly body, Jesus could be in only one place at a time. Through the Spirit, God’s presence lives within every believer, everywhere, at once.

## 5. He lives in you

“Know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you?” (1 Corinthians 6:19). In the Old Testament, God’s presence filled a temple of stone. Now He makes His home in ordinary people who trust in Jesus.

## 6. He always points to Jesus

“He shall not speak of himself… He shall glorify me” (John 16:13–14). This is one of the most helpful truths for learning to recognize His voice. The Holy Spirit never leads you away from Jesus or contradicts the Scriptures He inspired. If a thought draws you closer to Christ and lines up with the Word, that’s a good sign it’s from Him.

## 7. He prays for you when you can’t

“We know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered” (Romans 8:26). On the days you have no words, you are still being prayed for.

## How to begin knowing Him

Jesus made it remarkably simple: “how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?” (Luke 11:13). You can start today with a short breath prayer. Breathe in and pray, *“Spirit of God…”* Breathe out and pray, *“…breathe on me.”*
